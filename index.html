Consultation Prep Guidelines

Follow these simple prep recommendations to make your face-to-face appointment seamless:

1
Freshly Cleaned Hair

Wash your hair 24 hours before your session using a clarifying shampoo. Avoid applying heavy oils or thick styling creams.

2
Wear Natural Texture

Let your hair dry naturally so your stylist can immediately examine your natural bounce pattern, volume density, and curl structures.

AI Hair Diagnostic Profiler

Answer a few questions regarding your hair chemistry and density to generate a biometric health profile. Receive styled cut recommendations and instant price calculations.

Step 1 of 3

What is your natural hair texture?

Straight

Fine, medium, or coarse strands with no wave patterns.

Wavy

Loose S-shaped waves that lay flatter near the scalp.

Curly

Defined springy loops with moderate frizz vulnerability.

Coily / Afro

Tight helical coils or Z-pattern coils with high density.

Step 2 of 3

How does your hair behave throughout the day?

Dry / Frizzy

Feels coarse, absorbs moisture quickly, breaks easily.

Balanced

Easy to style, soft touch, holds curls or straight lines well.

Oil Prone

Feels heavy or flat near the roots, requires daily washes.

Step 3 of 3

What chemical treatments are currently in your hair?

Virgin / Natural

No color dye, bleaches, chemical straighteners, or perms.

Colored / Highlights

Regular tinting, semi-permanent coloring, or highlights.

Bleached / Damaged

High-lift blonde, global bleach, double process, or split ends.
